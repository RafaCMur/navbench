What PyRoboSim does here

Everything here was run in PyRoboSim, using its own actions for picking up, putting down, opening and closing. There is no physics in them. The soda just moves along with the robot, and when it is put down PyRoboSim picks a free spot inside the trash can. A closed trash can hides what is inside.

PyRoboSim does not let a robot open something while it is holding an object. So both robots first drive to the trash can and open it, then fetch the soda. I set each action to take one second. A* drives the same three routes for every seed. RRT* plans its three drives again for each seed.

The world, the robot, the objects and both planners come from PyRoboSim by Sebastian Castro (MIT license). PyRoboSim planned the runs in Python, and this page only replays them.
