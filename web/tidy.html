What PyRoboSim does here

Everything on this page was run in PyRoboSim with its own actions for picking, placing, opening and closing. Picking and placing are bookkeeping, not physics: the soda moves with the robot, and when it is put down PyRoboSim picks a free spot inside the trash can. A closed trash can hides what is inside it.

PyRoboSim does not let a robot open something while it holds an object, so both robots first drive to the trash can and open it, then fetch the soda. Each action takes one second here. A* drives the same routes for every seed. RRT* plans each of its three drives with the chosen seed.

The world, the robot, the objects and both planners come from PyRoboSim by Sebastian Castro (MIT license). The runs were planned by PyRoboSim in Python and this page only replays them.
